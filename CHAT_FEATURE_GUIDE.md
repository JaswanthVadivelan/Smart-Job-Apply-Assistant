# 💬 Chat Feature Guide

## Overview
The Smart Job Apply Assistant now includes an **interactive chat feature** that lets you refine your generated email through natural conversation!

---

## 🎯 How to Use

### Step 1: Generate Initial Email
1. Upload your job poster and resume
2. Click "Generate Application Email"
3. Review the generated email

### Step 2: Refine with Chat
Below the generated email, you'll find the **"Chat to Refine Your Email"** section.

Simply type what changes you want, and the assistant will update the email automatically!

---

## 💡 What You Can Ask

### **Tone Changes**
- "Make it more formal"
- "Make it more enthusiastic"
- "Make it sound more professional"
- "Change the tone to be more passionate"

### **Length Adjustments**
- "Make it shorter"
- "Make it more concise"
- "Make it longer and more detailed"
- "Make it brief"

### **Add Specific Content**
- "Add a line about my internship experience"
- "Mention my projects"
- "Add that I'm available to start immediately"
- "Include my experience with real-world applications"

### **Greeting Changes**
- "Change greeting to Dear Sir/Madam"
- "Make the greeting more formal"

### **Custom Additions**
You can also add custom text:
- "I have completed a 6-month internship at XYZ Company"
- "I am particularly interested in machine learning roles"
- "I can relocate if needed"

---

## 🛠️ Features

### ✏️ **Direct Editing**
You can also edit the email directly in the text area if you prefer manual changes.

### 🔄 **Reset to Original**
Click the "Reset to Original" button to go back to the first generated email.

### ↩️ **Undo**
Made a mistake? Click "Undo" to revert to the previous version.

### 🗑️ **Clear Chat**
Clear your conversation history to start fresh.

### 📥 **Download**
Download your final email as a text file.

## 🎨 Supported Modifications

| Request Type | Example | Result |
|--------------|---------|--------|
| **Formal** | "Make it more formal" | Removes contractions, adds professional language |
| **Enthusiastic** | "Make it excited" | Adds enthusiasm, exclamation marks |
| **Shorter** | "Make it concise" | Creates brief, to-the-point version |
| **Longer** | "Make it detailed" | Adds more context and elaboration |
| **Internship** | "Add internship" | Mentions internship experience |
| **Projects** | "Mention projects" | Adds line about projects |
| **Availability** | "Add availability" | States immediate availability |
| **Custom** | Any text | Adds your custom text to the email |

---

## 💡 Tips

1. **Be Specific**: The more specific your request, the better the result
2. **One Change at a Time**: Make one modification, review, then make another
3. **Use Undo**: Don't worry about mistakes - you can always undo
4. **Combine Requests**: You can say "Make it more formal and add my internship experience"
5. **Manual Edit**: For precise changes, edit directly in the text area

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Generate your email
2. Scroll down to the chat section
3. Type: "Make it more formal"
4. Click "Apply Changes"
5. Review the updated email
6. Continue refining until perfect!
